## Hello there. Welcome to my website. <span style="font-size: 0.5em;"></span>

A little bit about myself :
- At September 2025, I will be joining [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.com/) as a Research Engineer in the [Foundational Research](https://deepmind.google/research/) team.
- I'm currently holding multiple visiting researcher appointments at [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/), [MILA](https://mila.quebec/en), and [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/). 
- I did my Master's at the University of Oxford, where I was affiliated with the Oxford Applied Theoretical Machine Learning Group ([OATML](https://oatml.cs.ox.ac.uk/)), supervised by [Prof. Yarin Gal](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/yarin.gal/website/). My thesis, ["Fine-Tuning Large Language Models to Abstain Appropriately via Semantic Entropy"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17234) was recently accepted to _NeurIPS Safe Generative AI Workshop 2024. Additionally, I collaborated with [Prof. Michael Bronstein](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=UU3N6-UAAAAJ&hl=en)on temporal graph learning. Our work ["Enhancing the Expressivity of Temporal Graph Networks via Source-Target Identification"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03596) was accepted to _NeurIPS Symmetry and Geometry in Neural Representations Workshop 2024_ as an **oral** presentation. 
- Prior to Oxford, I did my Bachelor's at the University of Cambridge, where I was the **valedictorian** of my cohort. 
- Other than machine learning, I have extensive experience (5 years) in software engineering, having spent time at [XTX Markets](https://www.xtxmarkets.com/) and [Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/).
